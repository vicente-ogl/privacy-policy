Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 15, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Dark Counter app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Vicente Ogl (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Commercial application. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Overview

The Application is designed to be privacy-first. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information, user data, or usage analytics.

Information Collection and Use

The Application functions primarily offline. We do not use any tracking cookies, third-party analytics (like Google Analytics or Firebase), or advertising networks.

Google Fonts

The Application uses Google Fonts to display text. By default, this library may fetch fonts from Google servers if they are not already cached on your device.

  • This process involves your device sending a standard HTTP request to Google's servers.
  • As a result, your IP address may be visible to Google during this specific request for the purpose of delivering the font file.
  • Google's use of this data is governed by their Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The Application does not require any dangerous or runtime permissions.

  • It does not access your Camera, Photos, Contacts, or Microphone.
  • It does not access your precise Location (GPS).

The Application may use the WAKE_LOCK permission automatically to keep the screen on during gameplay, ensuring an uninterrupted experience. This does not access any data.

Data Retention

Since we do not collect any user data, we have no data to retain or delete. All game state (like life totals) is stored temporarily in your device's memory while the app is running.

Children's Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. We will notify you of any changes to our Privacy Policy by posting the new Privacy Policy here.
